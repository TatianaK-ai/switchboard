title: Laptop will not charge
path: hardware
privileged: false
---
1. Confirm the charger LED state and whether the dock is involved. Docks fail far more
   often than chargers.
2. Bypass the dock: plug the charger directly into the laptop.
3. Try a different known-good outlet.
4. Drain-reset: hold the power button 30 seconds with the charger removed, then
   reconnect.

If none of that works the unit needs a hardware swap. File a ticket for Field Services
with the asset tag and the office - do not promise a timeline, stock varies by site.
